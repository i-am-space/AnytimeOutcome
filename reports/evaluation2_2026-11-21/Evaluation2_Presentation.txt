Backup 7 · Ownership rules that prevent stale reads
The producer owns model/KV writes; a consumer owns its completed snapshot
State
Required rule
Before publication
Prefix event completed; matching generation/context
Partial reply
Independent snapshot; valid length excludes uncomputed tail
During response
Retain snapshot and producer storage until readers finish
Before next model/KV reuse
Join response and submitted tail writes
Miss, prefill, request end, exit
Retire outstanding work before clearing or reusing state
Overlap changes ordering, not the obligation to finish all outstanding accesses.
